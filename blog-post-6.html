👠 The Footwear Report


The shoe trends I'm seeing everywhere for Spring/Summer 2026—and the styles worth adding to your wishlist.

June 3, 2026 • 5 min read
View this post on Instagram

A post shared by Gabriela Gregoris (@gabby_fashioninspo)

As we transition into the warmer months, it's time to talk about the foundation of every great outfit: footwear. From sporty hybrid flats to retro classics, the shoe trends for Spring/Summer 2026 are all about balancing ease, playfulness, and refined elegance.

This season is proof that you don't have to sacrifice comfort to make a statement. Designers are merging practical silhouettes with high-fashion details, giving us styles that look elevated but feel amazing to wear all day long.

Here is my curated edit of the shoe trends I'm seeing everywhere—and the specific styles worth adding to your wishlist.

👠 The Footwear Trends

👟 01. THE SNEAKERINA

The sneakerina blends the comfort of a sneaker with the elegance of a ballet flat. This hybrid silhouette has quickly become one of the most talked-about footwear trends of 2026, and I can see why. It feels fresh, comfortable, and surprisingly versatile.

Why I Love It

  • Comfortable for everyday wear
  • Works with denim and dresses
  • Adds a fashion-forward touch to basic outfits

Puma Speedcat Ballet

Adidas Taekwondo Mei Ballet

📐 02. GEOMETRIC HEELS

Geometric heels are proof that shoes can be wearable art. From sculptural wedges to architectural shapes, these styles instantly elevate an outfit without requiring much effort.

Why I'm Watching This Trend

I love how a statement heel can transform even the simplest outfit. Pair them with tailored trousers, linen pieces, or a slip dress for an elevated look.

Zara Leather Wedges

Steve Madden Suede

⚓ 03. THE PREP REVIVAL

Classic prep-inspired footwear is making a major comeback. Loafers, driving shoes, and boat shoes are appearing everywhere from runway collections to street style looks.

Why I Think It's Trending

These styles feel polished, timeless, and easy to incorporate into an existing wardrobe. If you're drawn to quiet luxury or coastal-inspired fashion, this trend is worth trying.

Sperry x Aritzia Boat Shoe

J.Crew Driving Loafers

💎 04. HIGH-GLOSS JELLIES

Jelly shoes are back, but this time they feel much more refined. Modern versions feature sleek silhouettes and glossy finishes that make them feel surprisingly chic.

My Take

This trend is fun, playful, and perfect for summer vacations. It's also a great way to add personality to a simple warm-weather outfit.

Gap Factory Jelly Sandals

Gap Jelly Flats

🔗 05. DUAL CHROME

Mixed metallic finishes are replacing single-tone metallics this season. Silver and gold accents together create a versatile statement that works with almost everything.

Why I Love It

Dual chrome footwear gives you the freedom to mix metals throughout your outfit without overthinking it.

Dream Pairs Pump

DSW Slingback Pump

🧶 06. ARTISANAL WEAVES

Handcrafted textures continue to influence fashion in 2026. Woven footwear brings a relaxed luxury feel that works beautifully with spring and summer dressing.

Why It's On My Radar

I love how woven details add texture and interest while still feeling timeless.

Zara Braided Flats

Dolce Vita Woven Slides

👟 07. GUM SOLE CLASSICS

Retro sneakers remain a wardrobe staple, and gum sole styles continue to dominate casual fashion.

Why I Recommend Them

If you're only investing in one trend from this list, this is probably the easiest one to wear. They're comfortable, versatile, and pair effortlessly with most outfits.

Adidas Spezial

Puma Palermo

💡 Gabby's Pro Tip

Don't focus on buying every trend. Instead, choose one statement pair that fits your existing wardrobe and wear it with your favorite basics.

  • ✨ Love minimal outfits? Try geometric heels.
  • ✨ Wear lots of denim? Add a sneakerina.
  • ✨ Prefer timeless pieces? Invest in prep-inspired loafers.
  • ✨ Need versatility? Gum sole classics work with almost everything.

The best trend is the one you'll actually wear.

🌸 Final Thoughts
One thing I always remind myself is that trends should inspire your wardrobe, not completely replace it.

Whether you're drawn to sneakerinas, prep-inspired loafers, woven textures, or retro sneakers, focus on choosing styles that fit your lifestyle and personal aesthetic. The goal isn't to wear every trend—it's to find the ones that genuinely work for you.
